Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026.

What we collect

Information you submit in our forms: name, contact details, business and project information, and any documents you upload. We also collect basic analytics (pages visited, device type) if you accept analytics.

Why we collect it

To evaluate partnership applications, respond to project enquiries and general messages, and to improve this website.

Storage & security

Submissions are stored in a secured database with row-level access controls. Uploaded documents are kept in private storage accessible only to authorised administrators.

Retention

Applications and enquiries are kept for up to 24 months after last contact, unless a project or partnership agreement requires longer. You can request deletion earlier.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers that host or operate this website, under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your information by emailing hello@garudawnx.com. This policy is designed with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.

Cookies & analytics

We use essential storage for site functionality and, if enabled, Google Analytics 4 to understand usage in aggregate.
