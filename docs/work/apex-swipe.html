CASE STUDY · LIVE-COMMERCE APP

Apex Swipe

Apex Swipe is a live-commerce app where sellers and creators showcase products in live video sessions, and viewers interact in real time and place bids with a single swipe. We designed and built the platform — iOS and Android apps plus the website — for Apexswipe Private Limited, Bengaluru.

CLIENT

Apexswipe Private Limited, Bengaluru

CATEGORY

Live-commerce app

STATUS

Live

Visit apexswipe.com→App Store→Google Play→

THE CHALLENGE

Online shopping is mostly static: product photos, listings and a checkout button. Buyers can't see a product in action or ask the seller a question in the moment, and sellers and creators have no simple way to sell to an engaged live audience. The brief was to make buying feel live and social, while keeping bidding fast enough to happen mid-stream and safe enough that nobody bids by accident.

OUR SOLUTION

A mobile-first platform built around live sessions. Sellers and creators host live drops; viewers watch, chat and react in real time; and the signature swipe-to-bid control lets them bid with one deliberate drag of a slider — no typing, no pop-up confirmations and no accidental taps. The highest bid wins, payment runs through UPI, and the order moves straight into shipping with tracking updates on WhatsApp.

KEY FEATURES

  • Live video sessions hosted by sellers and creators
  • Swipe-to-bid slider that confirms only on a deliberate drag
  • Real-time chat and interaction in every live session
  • Live discovery feed by category — sneakers, collectibles, watches, vintage and tech
  • UPI checkout through Razorpay; card details are never stored
  • Shipping through Shiprocket with order tracking on WhatsApp
  • Seller live studio with instant UPI payouts
  • Apex Shield buyer protection
